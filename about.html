About ChargeNear

Find EV chargers near any address.

A free, open-source map of public EV charging stations, built so hotels and Airbnb hosts can show guests where to charge.

Open the map Embed on your site

What it does

ChargeNear shows public chargers around your location or any address you search, using community data from Open Charge Map.

  • Filter fastBy network, Level 2 or DC fast, and power output.
  • Walking distanceShow a 5 min, 15 min, or 1 mile circle around a property.
  • One-tap directionsOpen any charger in Google Maps or share a link to it.

For hotels and Airbnb hosts

No on-site charger? You can still show EV-driving guests how close the nearest ones are. Enter your property's address, preview the map, and paste the code into your website. It's free and needs no account.

Privacy

            
ChargeNear has no accounts and doesn't store your location. If you allow location access, it's used in your browser only to center the map. To show results, your browser sends map positions and searches directly to Mapbox (maps and address search) and Open Charge Map (charger data). If Open Charge Map can't be reached directly, the request is retried through the corsproxy.io relay. Fonts and icons load from Google Fonts and cdnjs.

        


        

            
Open source

            
ChargeNear is MIT-licensed and contributions are welcome — whether that's code, design, or reporting a charger that's out of date. Missing or wrong charger data can be fixed directly on Open Charge Map, and the fix will show up here too.
